Frequently Asked Questions

How does TaskPulse integrate with Slack?

TaskPulse installs as a lightweight Slack bot. It sends automated, customizable check-in prompts to team members and posts consolidated digests in your designated team channels.

Is there a free trial for the Pro plan?

Yes! You can try the Pro Plan free for 14 days without entering a credit card. Simply sign in using your Google account to get started immediately.

How does testing Stripe Sandbox work?

Clicking the "Subscribe with Stripe" button opens our Stripe Checkout simulation modal configured with test credit card numbers (e.g. 4242...4242) so you can test full transaction workflows safely.
